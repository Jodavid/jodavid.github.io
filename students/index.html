Welcome to the Students & Advising page. I actively supervise and mentor students across undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral programs in the Department of Statistics (DEst) and the Graduate Program in Statistics (PPGEST) at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE).

Our research group focuses on Statistical Machine Learning, Deep Representation Learning, Polarimetric SAR (PolSAR) Remote Sensing, Generative AI & Autonomous Agents, and Applied Statistical Modeling.

Undergraduate Degree - Bachelor’s Thesis (Graduação - TCC)

  • Leandro Albuquerque da Luz

    • Degree: B.Sc. in Statistics · UFPE
    • Work / Monograph: Ensemble Fuzzy Naive Bayes: A New Approach for Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Image Classification (Ensemble Fuzzy Naive Bayes: Uma Nova Abordagem para Classificação de Imagens de Radar de Abertura Sintética - SAR)

    • Description: Formulated an ensemble strategy combining fuzzy parameter estimation with Naive Bayes classification to handle speckle noise and enhance land-cover discrimination in satellite radar imagery.

  • Marcelo Rauscher Rios

    • Degree: B.Sc. in Statistics · UFPE

    • Role: Advisor (Orientador)
    • Description: Evaluated state-of-the-art deep learning architectures specialized for tabular data structures (TabNet and TabR) in feature-extracted SAR polarimetric data.

📩 Prospective Students

Interested in joining our research group as an undergraduate researcher (PIBIC/PIBITI), undergraduate thesis student (TCC), master’s student (M.Sc.), or doctoral researcher (Ph.D.)?

Please send an email to jodavid.ferreira@ufpe.br with:

  1. Your updated Curriculum Vitae (or Lattes link);
  2. Academic transcripts (Histórico escolar);
  3. A brief statement of research interests and familiarity with R, Python, and statistical modeling.
