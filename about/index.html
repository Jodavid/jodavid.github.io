About Me

I am Jodavid Ferreira, Professor in the Department of Statistics (DEst) at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), Brazil.

Academic Background

  • Postdoctoral Fellowship in Decision Models and Health (PPGMDS) — Federal University of Paraíba (UFPB)
  • Ph.D. in Statistics — Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE)
  • M.Sc. in Statistics — Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE)
  • B.Sc. in Statistics — Federal University of Paraíba (UFPB)

Research Agenda

In my academic trajectory, I actively develop research in statistical learning, supervised classification, and unsupervised clustering/segmentation. My theoretical work spans:

  • Matrix-variate probability distributions & statistical inference
  • Hypothesis testing & information-theoretic distance measures
  • Polarimetric Synthetic Aperture Radar (PolSAR) remote sensing image processing
  • Vision Transformers (ViT) & deep representation learning

Research Laboratories & Affiliations

I actively contribute to and hold leadership roles in specialized academic research laboratories across UFPE and UFPB:

  • LMET-SR — Laboratório de Modelagem Espaço-Temporal para Sensoriamento Remoto (UFPE) · Vice-Coordinator
  • CASTLAB — Computational Agriculture Statistics Laboratory (UFPE) · Vice-Coordinator
  • LEEME — Laboratório de Estatística Espacial e Modelagem Estatística (UFPE) · Professor & Researcher
  • LEAPIG — Laboratório de Estatística Aplicada ao Processamento de Imagens e Geoprocessamento (UFPB) · Professor & Researcher
  • LABTEVE — Laboratório de Tecnologias para o Ensino Virtual e Estatística (UFPB) · Professor & Researcher

Open-Source & Computational Science

I am an advocate and enthusiast of Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS). I build computational tools in R and Python, creating packages and reproducible workflows for data science, remote sensing, and statistical modeling.

Industry Experience & AI Leadership

Alongside academic appointments, I have served as Technical Lead of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence in Brazilian technology startups:

  • Generative AI & LLM Systems: Architected production solutions utilizing foundation models (GPT-4, Gemini), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and fine-tuning pipelines.
  • Enterprise Data Science: Developed machine learning pipelines for predictive scoring, persona profiling with statistical metrics, and customer recommendation engines.
